Meet the doctor

Francisco Chumpitaz, DDS

Dedicated to creating healthy smiles through compassionate, personalized dental care.

Dr. Francisco Chumpitaz decided to become a dentist after seeing his father work closely with patients. Inspired by the opportunity to help others, he discovered that dentistry was the right career for him.

He received his DDS degree from Universidad Inca Garcilaso de la Vega in Peru and completed his Advanced Education in General Dentistry (AEGD) at Larkin Community Hospital. His favorite part of dentistry is seeing a patient’s smile return after treatment.
